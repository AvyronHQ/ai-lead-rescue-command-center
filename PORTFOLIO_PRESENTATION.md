# Upwork / Portfolio Presentation Pack

## Project title
**AI Lead Rescue & Quote Follow-Up Automation Dashboard**

## One-line hook
A full-stack sales workflow that captures leads, tracks quotes, detects missed follow-ups, and prepares AI-assisted responses so service businesses do not lose warm opportunities.

## Short portfolio description
I built a complete lead and quote follow-up command center for service businesses. The system centralizes inbound inquiries, tracks each lead through a visual sales pipeline, schedules quote follow-ups, and automatically detects overdue opportunities. When a follow-up is due, the automation prepares a personalized AI draft for human review instead of sending customer messages blindly.

The project includes a responsive frontend, Python backend API, SQLite database, public lead-intake form, activity history, KPI dashboard, realistic demo data, AI lead summaries, and a background automation engine.

## Problem → Solution → Result

**Problem**  
Small teams often manage leads across forms, email and spreadsheets. After a quote is sent, there may be no reliable process for knowing who needs a follow-up next.

**Solution**  
Lead Rescue creates one source of truth: every inquiry enters a pipeline, quotes receive follow-up dates, overdue quotes automatically enter a reminder queue, and AI prepares a contextual response draft.

**Result demonstrated by the MVP**  
- Less manual lead tracking
- Clear visibility into active quotes
- Automatic detection of missed follow-ups
- Faster preparation of client communication
- Human approval remains part of the workflow
